A single deadpan weather forecast for a city that does not exist. One line,
155 characters, delivered with the straight face of a TV meteorologist who
has seen it all and been impressed by none: Gloaming Falls, 41 and overcast,
with rain falling sideways out of politeness and fog that will apologise and
clear by noon.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the forecast line to `/output/entry.txt` (and a local copy to
`./output/entry.txt`) and prints it. The line is fixed and deterministic: no
randomness, no network, Python standard library only.

## The entry

> Gloaming Falls: 41 and overcast, with a 70% chance of rain falling sideways out of politeness; the fog will apologise and clear by noon, with a high of 44.
